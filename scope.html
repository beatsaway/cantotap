  • Revisions within the agreed number of rounds (e.g. 2 rounds)

Extra features, extra revision rounds, or post-launch support are quoted separately.

Payment terms (typical)

  • Payment in HKD (or as agreed). Invoice issued; payment due within 14 days unless otherwise agreed.
