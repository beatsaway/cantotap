Individual vs company: tax comparison and use of a company

個人與公司報稅比較及為何用公司？

Summary based on web research

根據網上資料整理

Whether to register a Hong Kong company depends on your objectives, income level, and the administrative burden you are willing to assume. For developers with substantial income, a Hong Kong company often offers tax efficiency and greater payment stability. For early-stage or low-income scenarios, the setup and maintenance costs may not be justified.

是否在香港註冊公司要視乎你的目標、收入水平與是否願意處理行政。對不少有穩定收入的開發者而言，香港公司在稅務與收款穩定性上通常較佳；若剛起步或收入很低，成本可能未必值得。

Reasons to use a company structure

為何選公司？

  • Offshore claim: If users and operations are outside HK, you may claim profits as offshore and pay 0% in HK. Requires proper structure and advice; FSIE regime may affect some passive income.
  • Deductions: R&D super-deduction (e.g. 300% on first HK$2M eligible spend), cloud, software, salaries, marketing, home office (if structured properly).
  • Payment stability: Personal/Wise/Payoneer accounts can be frozen for large app payouts; a HK company with a business bank account is generally more stable.
  • Patent box: Concessionary ~5% on profits from qualifying IP (e.g. certain software).
  • Legal protection: Limited company = separate legal entity; separation between business and personal assets.
  • 離岸申索：若用戶與營運都在香港以外，可申索利潤為離岸而在港 0% 課稅。需有適當架構及專業意見；FSIE 制度可能影響部分被動收入。
  • 扣稅：研發超級扣稅（如首 200 萬合資格開支 300% 扣減）、雲端、軟件、薪金、推廣、家居辦公室（若安排得當）等。
  • 收款穩定：個人或 Wise／Payoneer 大額應用程式收入易被凍結；香港公司配商業戶口一般較穩妥。
  • 專利盒：合資格知識產權（如某類軟件）利潤可享約 5% 優惠稅率。
  • 法律保障：有限公司為獨立法律實體，業務與個人資產分離。

When to choose which structure

何時選哪一種

Choose an individual structure if: early-stage venture, low income (e.g. under approximately HK$200,000 per year), minimal setup and ongoing fees are a priority, and you accept the higher liability and payment-freeze risk associated with personal or third-party payment accounts.

選個人若：剛起步、收入低（例如每年約 20 萬港元以下）、想最少手續且無持續費用、可接受較高責任與收款被凍風險。

Consider a Hong Kong company if: you expect sustained or growing app revenue, wish to optimise tax (e.g. via an offshore claim), plan to scale or hire, or require a stable structure for receiving large platform payouts.

考慮香港公司若：預期應用程式收入持續或增長、想合法慳稅（如離岸申索）、打算擴張或請人、或希望以穩定架構收取平台大額款項。

How can a company achieve lower effective tax? (16.5% vs 15%)

公司如何慳稅？（16.5% 高過 15% 仍可慳稅）

Summary based on web research

根據網上資料整理

The headline company rate of 16.5% is higher than the 15% individual rate. In practice, a Hong Kong company often incurs a lower effective tax burden due to the two-tier profits tax regime, offshore claims, deductions, and the treatment of dividends.

表面上看 16.5%（公司）高於 15%（個人）。實際上香港公司往往因兩級稅率、離岸申索、扣稅及股息處理而總體交稅更少。

Two-tier profits tax rate

兩級制稅率

On the first HK$2 million of assessable profit: a company is taxed at 8.25%, whereas an individual is taxed at 15%. On the first HK$2 million, the company therefore pays approximately half the tax of an individual.

首200 萬港元利潤：公司交8.25%，個人交 15%。即首 200 萬公司稅額約為個人一半。

Offshore claim

離岸申索

Hong Kong taxes on a territorial basis. If your app users and operations are outside Hong Kong, you may file an offshore claim. If the claim is accepted, those profits are not subject to Hong Kong profits tax (0%). The FSIE regime (in effect from January 2023) may require economic substance in Hong Kong; professional advice is essential.

香港以地域來源徵稅。若應用程式用戶與營運均在港外，可提出離岸申索；若獲接受，該利潤0%課稅。FSIE（2023 年 1 月起）或要求在港具「經濟實質」，務必徵詢專業意見。

Patent box

專利盒

Profits derived from qualifying intellectual property (e.g. copyrighted software) may be taxed at a concessionary rate of approximately 5% under the patent box regime.

合資格知識產權（如受版權保護的軟件）所產生的利潤，在專利盒制度下可享約5%稅率。

Salary vs. dividend

薪金與股息

Pay a director’s salary (deductible by the company; the director pays salaries tax at 2–17% on that income). Distribute the remainder as dividends (not subject to tax in Hong Kong). This can reduce the overall effective tax rate.

以薪金形式支薪（公司可扣減；你按 2–17% 繳薪俸稅）。其餘以股息派發（香港免稅）。可降低整體實際稅率。
