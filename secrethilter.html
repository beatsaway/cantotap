Secret Hitler — Step-by-Step Guide

《秘密希特拉》玩法

1) What is the game?
It’s a team game about passing good or bad laws while trying to figure out who is on each team.
呢個係一個分隊合作嘅遊戲，大家會通過好或壞嘅法案，同時估下邊個係自己隊友。
2) How many players?
5–10 players.
5–10 個玩家。
3) Roles (keep them secret!)
Liberals: try to pass good (liberal) laws.
Fascists: try to pass bad (fascist) laws.
Secret Hitler: a special fascist who hides.
自由派：想通過好法案。
法西斯：想通過壞法案。
秘密希特拉：特別嘅法西斯，要藏身份。
4) Setup
Shuffle and deal each player one secret role card.
Give each player a party card (Liberal or Fascist).
Put the policy cards in a draw pile.
洗勻並派一張身份卡畀每人。
再派一張陣營卡（自由派 / 法西斯）。
準備好法案牌堆。
5) Each round has 3 big steps
Step A — Choose a President: The next player in order becomes President.
步驟 A — 揀總統： 輪到嘅玩家做總統。
Step B — Choose a Chancellor: The President picks a Chancellor. Everyone votes Yes/No.
步驟 B — 揀總理： 總統揀一個總理，全體投票 贊成/反對。
Step C — Pass a Law: President draws 3 policy cards, discards 1. Chancellor chooses 1 of the remaining 2 to pass.
步驟 C — 通過法案： 總統抽 3 張法案牌，丟 1 張。總理喺剩低 2 張入面揀 1 張通過。
6) Winning the game
Liberals win if they pass 5 Liberal laws.
Fascists win if they pass 6 Fascist laws.
Fascists also win if Secret Hitler becomes Chancellor after 3 Fascist laws are passed.
自由派贏：通過 5 條自由派法案。
法西斯贏：通過 6 條法西斯法案。
法西斯亦會贏：當通過 3 條法西斯法案後，秘密希特拉被選做總理。
7) Talking & Guessing (very important)
After each round, talk about what happened. Who do you trust? Why?
每回合之後可以討論：你信邊個？點解？
8) Tips for kids
Be kind and keep it fun.
Don’t shout; just explain your reasons.
It’s just a game — no real enemies!
友善啲，玩得開心最重要。
唔好大叫，用理由講清楚。
呢個只係遊戲，唔係真敵人！
Advanced Tips
EN:
  • After 3 Fascist laws, if Secret Hitler is elected Chancellor, Fascists win immediately.
  • Failed elections: if 3 governments fail in a row, the top policy is enacted automatically.
  • Policy deck: if there are fewer than 3 cards, shuffle the discard pile into a new draw pile.
  • Presidential powers: some Fascist laws unlock special actions (check your board).
粵:
  • 通過 3 條法西斯法案後，如果秘密希特拉當選總理，法西斯即刻勝利。
  • 連續 3 次政府組成失敗：自動翻開並通過牌堆頂一張法案。
  • 法案牌不足 3 張：將棄牌堆洗勻重新成為抽牌堆。
  • 總統權力：部分法西斯法案會解鎖特別行動（睇版圖）。
