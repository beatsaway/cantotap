Hedera (HBAR)

What is Hedera?

Hedera = a public network (Hashgraph consensus, not Ethereum-style).

Coin: HBAR — used for fees (gas) and staking.

NFTs: Supported via HIP-412. You mint on Hedera and pay fees in HBAR.

Partnerships & council

Google (Council + node), IBM (supply chain), LG, Tata, and others. Enterprise focus: high-speed, low-cost, secure. Governing Council of up to 39 organizations (e.g. Avery Dennison, Boeing, Deutsche Telekom, FIS, Zain). Hashgraph consensus — no traditional PoW/PoS.

Recent

Accenture NAV AI on Hedera; HBAR Foundation, Swirlds Labs, Hashgraph Association fund developers. Early 2026: focus on enterprise adoption.

Hedera for artists

Pros

  • Very low, predictable fees
  • Fast — settles in seconds
  • Ecosystem: SentX, Kabila, GoMint, Mintbar.xyz (HIP-412, IPFS)

Cons

  • Smaller NFT audience vs Ethereum, Solana
  • Wallets: HashPack, Blade (not MetaMask by default)
  • Less artist buzz than Zora/Base

When it makes sense: You want cheap, fast minting and don’t mind a smaller collector base; or you’re already in the Hedera ecosystem; or you care about sustainability.

Earning HBAR

You don’t earn from minting (you pay a small fee). You earn when you sell the NFT and from royalties on resales.

MintNo — you (or buyer) pay HBAR
First saleYes — buyer pays you in HBAR (or stablecoin)
ResalesYes — % royalty in HBAR if marketplace supports it

Gallery idea: upload site + business models

Art-first site: free up to 5 images; after that, upload fee. Behind the scenes: fee → HBAR → mint NFT on Hedera. No “NFT” in the UI.

Ways to make money

  • Upload fee margin (charge more than mint cost)
  • Conversion spread (fiat → HBAR)
  • Subscriptions (Pro/Creator)
  • Sale commission when they sell
  • Royalty share, featured placement, premium features, B2B

Hashflip learnings (art gallery / auction)

Hashflip = Hedera raffles + coin flip; simple HBAR transfers, Hedera PRNG for fair draw.

  • Keep money flow simple (HBAR transfer = bid/ticket)
  • Use Hedera PRNG for raffles and tie-breakers
  • Low HBAR entry, live feed, creator-led
  • Integrate SentX/Kabila; offer auction + raffle + buy-now
  • Clear fees; “Built on Hedera” for trust

Cost to build Hedera art gallery

Build: ~$0–2k (DIY) or $5k–30k (with help). Per upload: ~$0.02–0.03 (mint + storage). Format: 800×1024 WebP works well.

Business models

Upload fee after N free; subscription; featured placement; sale commission; royalty share; tiered resolution; B2B; tips.

Transparent gallery model

Art lasts forever; each piece purchasable. Buyer can show name on piece (optional) and leave a comment. Platform takes clear %; artists/buyers choose HBAR or USD with real-time rate. “We use profit from fees to pay for hosting all artworks.”

Flow

Artist uploads → mint on Hedera + IPFS → list. Artist sets price (HBAR or USD). Buyer pays → platform fee → rest to artist. Buyer opts in for name + comment. Platform reports fees → hosting.

Who uploads & removal

Best fit: Real artists, galleries (permanent portfolio, credibility, one link). Also cultural/meme/coders. Incentives: “lasts forever,” ownership, discovery, sell later.

Removal: Offer “Hide from gallery” (delist on your site only). Optional “Burn” NFT (token gone; IPFS may remain). State in ToS.
